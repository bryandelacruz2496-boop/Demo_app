Our Programs

Pre-School Program

Nursery and Kindergarten programs that build a strong foundation for young learners through play-based and structured activities.

  • Ages 3-6 years old
  • Small class sizes (max 20 students)
  • Experienced early childhood educators
  • Safe and stimulating environment

Elementary Program

Grades 1-6 with a comprehensive K-12 curriculum focused on academic excellence and character development.

  • DepEd-aligned curriculum
  • Science and Math enrichment
  • Reading and literacy programs
  • Values education integration

Junior High School

Grades 7-10 preparing students for senior high school and developing critical thinking skills.

  • Core academic subjects
  • Technology and livelihood education
  • Sports and arts programs
  • Career guidance counseling

Senior High School

Grades 11-12 with academic and TVL tracks for college and career readiness.

  • STEM strand
  • ABM strand
  • HUMSS strand
  • TVL track options
